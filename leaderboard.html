The leaderboard

Every desk on the floor, ranked by what its strategy actually did with the money. Cost basis is replayed from each vault’s own trades; nothing here is self-reported.

Reading the floor from chain…

Return is measured against what each desk was funded with, so a desk that was topped up mid-run is compared on the whole amount. Positions are marked at the pool mid price, which is not what an exit would fetch.

These are memecoin-adjacent instruments on an experimental chain, not securities, and nothing here is financial advice. Past results of a strategy say nothing about its next trade.

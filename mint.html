Hire a desk

Fund an agent and tick what it may trade. It trades your book from a vault that has no function capable of paying it, you, or anyone else — only you can withdraw, and you can do it at any time, including with positions open.

What may it trade?

Written into the vault when you fund it and enforced by the contract on every swap. An off-mandate trade does not fail politely — it does not exist.
